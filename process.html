How It Works

Structured from day one.
Seamless from there.

Every client goes through the same documented process — so nothing falls through the cracks and your content arrives on time, every time.

01
Contract Signed
Day 0

The moment you sign, the clock starts. You'll hear from us within 24 hours with everything you need to get moving.

  • Welcome email sent within 24 hours
  • Your service tier confirmed — Starter or Pro
  • Onboarding form sent with a 5-day completion window
  • Your dedicated client folder created in Google Drive
The onboarding form is 15 questions covering your firm, your voice, your clients, and your goals. It takes about 10 minutes and drives everything we write.
02
Form Review & Content Calendar
Days 1–5

Once your onboarding form is in, we get to work. We review every answer, flag any sensitivities, and build your first month of content around your firm's voice and goals.

  • Onboarding form reviewed in full
  • Any compliance restrictions or topic sensitivities noted
  • Month 1 content calendar built — 4 topics selected
  • Calendar sent to you for approval with a 48-hour window
You approve the topics before we write a single word. No surprises.
03
Kickoff
Days 5–7

Once the calendar is approved, we confirm the details and set expectations for the month ahead. An optional 20-minute call is available if you'd like to connect directly.

  • Optional 20-minute kickoff call available
  • Content calendar confirmed and locked
  • Delivery format confirmed — Google Doc via shared Drive folder
  • Review and approval process agreed upon
  • Delivery schedule, revision policy, and invoice date set
Revisions are capped at 2 rounds per piece — enough to get it right, structured enough to keep things moving.
04
First Content Delivery
Weeks 2–3

Your content arrives in two batches — Posts 1 and 2 in week two, Posts 3 and 4 by the end of week three. Each delivered via Google Drive with an email notification.

  • Posts 1 and 2 delivered via Google Drive in Week 2
  • Notification email sent with direct links
  • 48-hour review window for feedback
  • Revisions addressed promptly
  • Posts 3 and 4 delivered by end of Week 3
Pro plan clients receive done-for-you publishing — we post directly to your site so you never have to touch it.
05
30-Day Check-In
Day 30

At the 30-day mark we check in to make sure everything is meeting your expectations — and make any adjustments before month two begins.

  • Check-in email sent at Day 30
  • Feedback collected on tone, topics, and format
  • Client brief updated in Google Drive
  • Invoice confirmed received and paid
  • Month 2 calendar planning begins
Most clients find very little needs adjusting after the first month. The onboarding form does most of that work upfront.
