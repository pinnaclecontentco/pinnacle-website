Revisions & Quality

Each piece includes up to two rounds of revisions. You review the draft, leave your feedback in the Google Doc, and we turn around revisions promptly — typically within 48 hours.

In practice, most pieces require very few changes. The onboarding process and content calendar approval are designed to get the direction right before writing begins, which means the drafts tend to land close to where they need to be.

We stand behind what we deliver. If a piece is not meeting the standard you expected, tell us specifically what is not working — and we will fix it. The two-revision rounds exist precisely for this.

If after the first month the content is consistently missing the mark, we want to know. The 30-day check-in is built into our process for exactly this reason — to catch anything that needs adjusting before it becomes a pattern.

We are transparent about our process: AI tools are used as part of the research and drafting workflow, with significant human editing, rewriting, and quality review at every stage. The final content is reviewed for accuracy, voice, tone, and compliance framing before it reaches you.

The result is content that reads like a knowledgeable professional wrote it — because a knowledgeable professional shaped, reviewed, and is accountable for every piece.
